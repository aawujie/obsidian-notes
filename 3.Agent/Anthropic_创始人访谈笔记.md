our safety values, it shows up through kind of the RSP, right? The RSP is gonna block them from doing what they want to do, and so it's a way to remind everyone over and over again, basically, to make safety a product requirement."
> （它也强制统一，因为如果组织的任何部分与我们的安全价值观不一致，它就会通过RSP显现出来，对吧？RSP会阻止他们做他们想做的事，所以这是一种一遍又一遍提醒每个人的方式，基本上，让安全成为产品需求。）

## 六、创立故事：从OpenAI到Anthropic

### "我们本不想创业"

**Dario** 坦言：
> "None of us wanted to found a company. We just felt like it was our duty... This is the way we're gonna make things go better with AI."
> （我们中没有人想创办公司。我们只是觉得这是我们的责任……这是我们让AI发展得更好的方式。）

**Chris** 补充：
> "I didn't want to leave. That was something that I think I was actually pretty reluctant to go along with, because I think, for one thing, I didn't know that it was good for the world to have more AI labs."
> （我不想离开。我认为我实际上很不愿意这样做，因为一方面，我不知道世界上有更多的AI实验室是好事。）

### 信任文化的建立

**Daniela** 谈到团队特质：
> "How much trust there is at this table... I think that is not, I mean, Tom, you were at other startups. I was never a founder before, but it's actually really hard to get a group of, like, a big group of people, to have like the same mission."
> （这张桌子周围有多少信任……我想这不是，Tom，你在其他初创公司待过。我以前从未是创始人，但实际上要让一大群人拥有相同的使命真的很难。）

**Dario** 强调：
> "Trust and credibility are more important than any particular policy."
> （信任和信誉比任何特定政策都重要。）

### 80%承诺

**Jared** 提到：
> "We did the the 80% pledge thing, and that was like a thing that everybody was just like, 'Yes, obviously we're gonna do this.'"
> （我们做了80%的承诺，这是每个人都只是说"是的，显然我们要这样做"的事情。）

## 八、迈向巅峰的竞赛

### "向顶部竞赛"

**Dario** 提出关键理念：
> "It's a bet on race to the top... The more successful Anthropic becomes as a company, the more incentive there is for people to copy the things that make us successful."
> （这是对向顶部竞赛的赌注……Anthropic作为一家公司越成功，人们就越有动力复制让我们成功的东西。）

**Jack** 形象比喻：
> "Export the seat belts... Sure, we'll build seat belts and everyone else can copy them. That's good."
> （出口安全带……当然，我们会制造安全带，其他人可以复制它们。这很好。）

### 客户驱动的安全

**Daniela** 观察到：
> "Customers don't want models that are hallucinating. They don't want models that are easy to jailbreak. They want models that are helpful and harmless... We're going with Claude because we know it's safer."
> （客户不想要产生幻觉的模型。他们不想要容易被破解的模型。他们想要有帮助且无害的模型……我们选择Claude是因为我们知道它更安全。）

### 避免"高尚的失败"

**Chris** 批评一种错误观念：
> "There's this narrative or this idea that maybe the virtuous thing is to almost like nobly fail, right? It's like you should go and like put safety, you should sort of demonstrate like in an impragmatic way so that you can sort of demonstrate your purity to the cause or something like this."
> （有一种说法或想法认为，也许高尚的事情几乎是高贵地失败，对吧？就像你应该去追求安全，你应该以一种不切实际的方式展示，这样你就能展示你对事业的纯粹性之类的东西。）

## 九、展望未来

### 可解释性的美丽

**Chris** 充满诗意地描述：
> "I think neural networks are beautiful and I think that there's a lot of beauty in them that we don't see... they're just full of amazing, beautiful structure... We're just starting to slowly unpack it, and it's incredible, and there's so much there."
> （我认为神经网络是美丽的，我认为其中有很多我们看不到的美……它们充满了令人惊叹的美丽结构……我们才刚刚开始慢慢解开它，它是不可思议的，那里有太多东西。）

### 生物学应用

**Daniela** 展望：
> "Imagining the future world of what AI is going to be able to do for people is it's impossible to not feel excited about that... Claude being able to help with vaccine development and cancer research and biological research is crazy."
> （想象AI未来能为人们做什么的世界，不可能不对此感到兴奋……Claude能够帮助疫苗开发、癌症研究和生物学研究是疯狂的。）

### 政府能力建设

**Jack** 提到：
> "Governments will set up new bodies to test and evaluate AI systems and they will actually be competent and good... it's kind of like governments have built these new embassies, almost, to deal with this new kind of class of technology."
> （政府将建立新的机构来测试和评估AI系统，它们实际上是有能力和好的……这几乎就像政府建立了这些新的大使馆来处理这种新型技术。）

### Dario的三大愿景

1. **可解释性**：
   > "Chris Olah is gonna be a future Nobel Medicine Laureate... a lot of these mental illnesses, the ones we haven't figured out, right, schizophrenia or the mood disorders, I suspect there's some higher level system thing going on."
   > （Chris Olah将成为未来的诺贝尔医学奖得主……很多我们还没搞明白的精神疾病，比如精神分裂症或情绪障碍，我怀疑有更高层次的系统问题。）

2. **AI用于生物学**：
   > "We saw a Nobel Prize in chemistry awarded for AlphaFold; remarkable accomplishment. We should be trying to build things that can help us create a hundred AlphaFolds."
   > （我们看到AlphaFold获得了诺贝尔化学奖；了不起的成就。我们应该尝试构建能帮助我们创造一百个AlphaFold的东西。）

3. **AI增强民主**：
   > "How can AI be a tool for freedom and self-determination? I think that one is earlier than the other two, but it's gonna be just as important."
   > （AI如何成为自由和自决的工具？我认为这一点比其他两点更早，但同样重要。）
