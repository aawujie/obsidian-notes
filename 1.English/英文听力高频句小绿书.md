## Unit 1：认识新朋友

1. You're coming to the party later tonight, right? — 今晚你会来派对吧？
2. Start getting ready. — 开始准备（出门）。
3. I literally just landed and got no fancy outfit. — 我刚下飞机，根本没有带正式的衣服。
4. No worries. / No biggies. / Don't worry about it. / It's okay. — 别担心。
5. Nice meeting you. / Nice to meet you. — 很高兴认识你。
6. My family lives 2 hours away. — 我家人住在离这里两小时的地方。
7. We moved here 10 years ago from Virginia. — 我们十年前从维吉尼亚州搬到这里。
8. I've never been there. — 我从没去过那里。
9. I would like to visit one day. — 我希望有一天能去那里。
10. There are a lot of museums and historical sites to visit. — 那里有很多博物馆和历史景点可以参观。
11. I'd imagine. — 我想也是。
12. Considering it's one of the oldest cities. — 毕竟那是最古老的城市之一。
13. What's your major? — 你的主修是什么？
14. I am in Beasley School of Law. — 我就读于 Beasley 法学院。
15. But I'm actually more interested in making music or playing pickleball. — 但我其实更喜欢制作音乐或玩匹克球。
16. I've always wanted to try pickleball. — 我一直想打打看匹克球。
17. It varies. / It depends. — 看情况 / 不一定。
18. I make mostly country and jazz music. — 我主要是做乡村音乐和爵士乐。
19. What brought you back to business school? — 你怎么会想回商学院？
20. Mostly I want to be an independent musician. — 主要是因为我想当独立音乐人。
21. My father encouraged me to come back to school. — 我父亲鼓励我回到学校。
22. You have a wise dad. — 你有一位很有智慧的爸爸。
23. I assume/suppose you are gonna practice law? — 我猜你将来会当执业律师吧？
24. I don't think that's gonna/going to happen. — 我觉得应该不会。
25. You should pursue/go after it. — 你应该追求那个目标。
26. Whatever it is that is true to your heart! — 做你真正热爱的事情！
27. Go grab some sliders and alcohol. — 去拿点小汉堡和酒。
28. I'll see you on the rooftop for the countdown later. — 等会倒数时屋顶见喔！

## Unit 2：家庭对话

1. How's your day been? / How are you today? — 你今天过得怎么样？
2. I'm a bit under the weather / unwell. / I'm not feeling well / too great. — 我身体有点不太舒服。
3. I'm just gonna/going to take some Theraflu. — 我打算服用一些感冒药水。
4. I have some Theraflu left in the kitchen cabinets. — 我厨房柜子里还有一些感冒药水。
5. I went to the (running) track with Mia earlier. — 我刚刚跟Mia去操场跑步。
6. One second / One moment / Hold on / Hold up / Wait. — 等一下。
7. I met up with Charles at the lab. — 我在实验室跟 Charles碰面。
8. We studied for the final (exam). — 我们有一起读书准备期末考。
9. It's been a long day. — 今天真是漫长的一天（今天发生好多事，所以很疲惫）。
10. I'll help with the dirty dishes while you make dinner! — 我来帮忙洗碗，你来做饭！
11. Who is on trash duty? — 今天是谁负责倒垃圾？
12. Let's just divide up the chores. — 但我们可以分配一下家务。
13. Take out the trash. — 倒垃圾。
14. I'll make us some spinach omelette. — 我来做菠菜欧姆蛋。
15. We'll have a pregame in the living room. — 我们星期五出去前可以在客厅来场小酌。
16. I was planning to switch up my room. — 我打算那时候重新布置房间。
17. No biggies / No worries. — 没什么大不了的。
18. You do you. — 做自己。
19. The faucet's been acting up lately. — 那水龙头最近又出状况了。
20. Is it leaking again? — 又漏水了吗？
21. Call the plumber. — 打电话叫水电工。
22. Adulting is hard. — 长大了真的不容易。
23. I feel you / I get you. — 我懂你。
24. On the bright side. — 往好的方面想。
25. Have a blast! — 玩得开心！

## Unit 3：餐厅点餐

1. What can I get started for you today? — 今天想要吃什么？
2. Can I get a bowl? — 我要一份碗装的餐点。
3. You got it. — 好的马上做。
4. What kind of rice / beans / protein? — 你要什么饭 / 豆子 / 蛋白质？
5. There's chicken, steak, carnitas, sofritas, and grilled veggies. — 我们有鸡肉、牛排、炖猪肉、素肉和烤蔬菜。
6. Can you do chicken and veggies, half and half? — 可以鸡肉和烤蔬菜各一半吗？
7. Any salsa? Mild, medium, or hot? — 要哪种莎莎酱？温和、中辣还是辣？
8. Guac is extra. — 酪梨酱需要额外收费。
9. Any chips and guac? — 要加玉米片和酪梨酱吗？
10. Cash or card? — 付现金还是刷卡？
11. You're all set. — 完成啰！
12. Have a good one/day! — 祝你今天愉快！
13. You as well! — 你也是！
14. Which flavor is the most popular? — 哪一个口味最受欢迎？
15. Chocolate peanut butter cup and triple chocolate. — 巧克力花生酱杯和三重巧克力。
16. Can I get a box of six? — 我可以要六个装一盒的吗？
17. Let me get those packed for you. — 我帮你打包。
18. Alrighty / Alright. — 好的。
19. Did you need the receipt printed? — 需要打印收据吗？
20. Be out in just a second. — 马上为你送上！

## Unit 4：购物与结账

1. Are you looking for anything specific/particular today? — 你今天有特别想找什么吗？
2. Just browsing/looking. — 只是随便看看。
3. Let me know if you need help with anything. — 如果需要帮忙随时告诉我。
4. Where is the fitting room? — 请问试衣间在哪里？
5. Straight down the hallway to the right. — 沿着走廊直走，在右边。
6. I'll go try it on. — 我去试穿看看。
7. I was hoping it comes in beige. — 我真希望有米色的款式。
8. Do you have this dress in a different color? OR Does this dress come in a different color? — 这件洋装有其他颜色吗？
9. I can help you with that. — 我可以帮你看看。
10. It is available online and in store. — 这款线上和门市都可以买到。
11. It comes in these colors. — 有这些颜色。
12. Are you looking for a going out dress or a (more) casual dress? — 你是想找正式外出穿的洋装还是比较休闲的洋装？
13. Do you want a maxi dress like this one or midi or mini? — 你想要像这样的长裙，还是中长裙或短裙？
14. Sleeveless, shortsleeves, longsleeves, strapless, or no preference? — （袖子要）无袖、短袖、长袖、平肩还是没特别偏好？
15. One is a sleeveless denim maxi with the buttons, another one is a midi crew neck black and white striped knit dress. — 第一件是无袖的牛仔长裙，带有扣子的设计；第二件是黑白条纹的圆领中长针织裙。
16. Where is the register? OR Where do I check out/pay? — 收银台在哪里？
17. Are you a member with us? — 您是我们的会员吗？
18. Your total's gonna be $87.63 today. — 今天的总金额是87.63美金。
19. Cash or card? — 你要支付现金还是刷卡？
20. When you're ready, it'll ask you a question on the screen. — 等您准备好后，萤幕上会有问题让您回答。
21. Did you need a bag? — 需要袋子吗？
22. Do you want a copy of your receipt? / Would you like a receipt? — 需要收据吗？

## Unit 5：住家维修

1. I'm calling because my AC is not working. — 我打电话来是因为我的冷气坏了。
2. I was wondering when you guys can come out and check it. — 我想询问你们什么时候能来检查一下？
3. It hasn't turned on since yesterday. — 它从昨天开始就没有再启动。
4. We'll send him in/out on Monday. — 我们会安排他周一过去。
5. The (AC) unit is out here in the backyard. — （冷气）设备在后院这边。
6. The compressor blew out and you are also low on refrigerant. — 压缩机坏掉了，而且你的冷媒也快不足了。
7. The total before taxes is gonna be $5,325.25. — 税前价格总共是5,325.25美金。
8. I listed all these problems out on an itemized estimate/statement. — 我把所有问题都列在一份有细项的估价单上了。
9. Holy moly! — 天啊！
10. It's gonna run into a lot of problems. — 未来可能会有很多问题。
11. I would recommend possibly looking at replacing your (AC) unit. — 我建议你考虑更换（冷气）设备。
12. In the long run — 从长远来看
13. I will send you this estimate for repairing your current (AC) unit. — 我会把修理目前（冷气）设备的估价单传给你。
14. I also noticed the thermostat seems off. — 我也发现恒温器好像不正常。
15. It's not reading the temperature correctly. — 因为它没有显示正确的温度。
16. Thanks for stopping by! — 谢谢你过来一趟！
17. Anytime / You're welcome! — 不客气！

## Unit 6：旅行与住宿

1. What are you here for? — 你来这里的目的是什么？
2. I'm here for a vacation. — 我来这里渡假。
3. Where are you staying? — 你要住在哪里？
4. I'm staying at a hotel for two nights then staying with my old coworker. — 我会先住两晚饭店，然后住在我的老同事家。
5. The address of the hotel is 216 Los Angeles Blvd. — 饭店的地址是洛杉矶大道216号。
6. How long do you plan to stay here? — 你预计在这里待多久？
7. In total 32 days. The first 2 days at the hotel and the rest with my old coworker. — 总共32天，前两天住饭店，之后都住在我老同事家。
8. How much money are you bringing in cash? — 你带了多少现金？
9. A little under $2,000. — 不到 2,000 美金。
10. No need to declare it. — 不需要申报。
11. What is your profession? — 你的职业是什么？
12. I am a marketing manager for a tech company. — 我是科技公司的行销主管。
13. You're all set! — 都好了！
14. Can I see the credit card that you used for your reservation? — 我可以看一下您用来预订的信用卡吗？
15. Here you go. — 这边给你。
16. Are we/you here to celebrate anything? — 我们来这是特别庆祝什么吗？
17. I was wondering if you have any suite upgrades available today? — 我想问一下今天有套房升级的选项吗？
18. One moment, please. — 请稍等一下。
19. I upgraded you to an ocean view suite on the 33rd floor with a Jacuzzi. — 我已经帮您升级到33楼有按摩浴缸的海景套房。
20. You'll also get free high-speed WiFi. — 您还可以免费使用高速无线网络。
21. Would you like a complimentary breakfast? — 您想要免费早餐吗？
22. Or extra points for your stay? — 还是额外累计点数呢？
23. You got it! — 好的，没问题。
24. The elevator is all the way across the lobby to the left. — 电梯在穿过大厅后的左边。
25. Did you need one or two key cards? — 你需要一张还是两张房卡？
26. Is there a gym here? — 这里有健身房吗？
27. What time does it open and close? (formal term: hours of operation) — 它几点开放和关闭？（营业时间）
28. I'm gonna go check it out. — 我等会去看看。
29. There is a $20 per day resort fee. — 每天有20美金的度假村费用。
30. Anything else I can help you with? — 还有什么我可以帮助您的吗？

## Unit 7：健康与医疗

1. What brings you here today? — 今天哪里不舒服？
2. I think I have a fever. — 我觉得我发烧了。
3. I will need your (picture) id. — 我需要您的身份证。
4. Date of birth? — 出生日期？
5. I also need the insurance card. — 我也需要你的医疗保险卡。
6. I have a picture of it. — 我有它的照片。
7. Fill out this top portion of the form, sign, and put down the date. — 请填写表格的上半部分，签名并填上日期。
8. Take a seat there and the doctor will call you. — 请在那边坐一下，医生会叫您。
9. I've been having a fever since yesterday, diarrhea too. — 我从昨天开始发烧，还有拉肚子。
10. At that point, I hadn't eaten anything for 30 hours. — 当时我已经大概30个小时没吃东西了。
11. I started getting chills. — 我开始发冷。
12. Any exposure to someone with a flu? — 你是否有接触有流感的人？
13. You're negative for covid, and also negative for the flu. — 你的 COVID 和流感检测结果都是阴性的。
14. Is it associated with anything that you ate? — 会不会是跟你吃的东西有关？
15. You work from home, right? — 你是在家工作，对吧？
16. I just had some leftover pasta from a restaurant. — 我只是吃了餐厅的意大利面剩菜。
17. You started feeling the symptoms right away? — 你马上就开始感觉到症状吗？
18. Are you allergic to any medications? — 你有对任何药物过敏吗？
19. No, not that I know of. — 据我所知是没有。
20. They took your blood pressure and temperature. — 他们有测了你的血压和体温。
21. Your heart rate is a bit elevated. — 你的心率有点偏高。
22. Not so much that we need to worry about. — 没有什么特别需要担心的。
23. Give them a stool sample. — 给他们送粪便样本。
24. We'll call you to let you know if you need any antibiotics. — 我们会打电话通知你是否需要抗生素。
25. Chills and headaches are primarily caused by dehydration. — 发冷和头痛主要是因为脱水造成的。
26. In the meanwhile, try to stay away from spicy food, greasy food, and dairy. — 在此同时，尽量避免辛辣食物、油腻食物和乳制品。
27. If you want to get something over the counter, electrolyte drinks will help you a lot. — 如果你想要买药房的非处方药，电解质饮料对你会有很大的帮助。
28. You might need IV fluids. — 你可能会需要打点滴。
29. Feel better! — 希望你早日康复！

## Unit 8：客诉与抱怨

1. Hello. How can I help you? — 喂，请问有什么可以协助您的？
2. I'm calling because the tire pressure light has been on. — 我打电话是因为轮胎气压灯亮了。
3. I did try to put air in the tires. — 我有尝试帮轮胎打气。
4. I didn't want to risk it. — 我不想冒险。
5. A 2-hour freeway drive — 两小时的高速公路车程
6. Who am I speaking to? — 请问怎么称呼您？
7. I apologize for the inconvenience. — 很抱歉造成您的不便。
8. We will send someone to tow your car away. — 我们会派人去拖您的车。
9. Where are you <span style="color:rgb(255, 77, 77)">located</span>? — 您的位置在哪里?

11. I see a Mcdonald's across the street. — 我看到对街有一家麦当劳。
12. What's the<span style="color:rgb(255, 77, 77)"> zip (code)</span>? — 邮递区号是什么？

16. Our flight is taking off in 4 hours. — 我们的航班四个小时后就要起飞了。
17. We can send a driver to pick you up. — 我们可以派司机来接您。

22. What would you suggest? — 你建议怎么做？
23. We can get you a ride/taxi. — 我们可以为你安排计程车。
24. I will figure that part out. — 我会自己解决这部分。
25. I might have to miss my flight. — 我可能会错过我的航班。
26. Do you compensate for hotel stays? — 你们会补偿旅馆住宿费用吗？
27. You can talk to the branch manager. — 你可以和门市经理联络。
