## Unit 1：認識新朋友

1. You're coming to the party later tonight, right? — 今晚你會來派對吧？
2. Start getting ready. — 開始準備（出門）。
3. I literally just landed and got no fancy outfit. — 我剛下飛機，根本沒有帶正式的衣服。
4. No worries. / No biggies. / Don't worry about it. / It's okay. — 別擔心。
5. Nice meeting you. / Nice to meet you. — 很高興認識你。
6. My family lives 2 hours away. — 我家人住在離這裡兩小時的地方。
7. We moved here 10 years ago from Virginia. — 我們十年前從維吉尼亞州搬到這裡。
8. I've never been there. — 我從沒去過那裡。
9. I would like to visit one day. — 我希望有一天能去那裡。
10. There are a lot of museums and historical sites to visit. — 那裡有很多博物館和歷史景點可以參觀。
11. I'd imagine. — 我想也是。
12. Considering it's one of the oldest cities. — 畢竟那是最古老的城市之一。
13. What's your major? — 你的主修是什麼？
14. I am in Beasley School of Law. — 我就讀於 Beasley 法學院。
15. But I'm actually more interested in making music or playing pickleball. — 但我其實更喜歡製作音樂或玩匹克球。
16. I've always wanted to try pickleball. — 我一直想打打看匹克球。
17. It varies. / It depends. — 看情況 / 不一定。
18. I make mostly country and jazz music. — 我主要是做鄉村音樂和爵士樂。
19. What brought you back to business school? — 你怎麼會想回商學院？
20. Mostly I want to be an independent musician. — 主要是因為我想當獨立音樂人。
21. My father encouraged me to come back to school. — 我父親鼓勵我回到學校。
22. You have a wise dad. — 妳有一位很有智慧的爸爸。
23. I assume/suppose you are gonna practice law? — 我猜你將來會當執業律師吧？
24. I don't think that's gonna/going to happen. — 我覺得應該不會。
25. You should pursue/go after it. — 你應該追求那個目標。
26. Whatever it is that is true to your heart! — 做妳真正熱愛的事情！
27. Go grab some sliders and alcohol. — 去拿點小漢堡和酒。
28. I'll see you on the rooftop for the countdown later. — 等會倒數時屋頂見喔！

## Unit 2：家庭對話

1. How's your day been? / How are you today? — 你今天過得怎麼樣？
2. I'm a bit under the weather / unwell. / I'm not feeling well / too great. — 我身體有點不太舒服。
3. I'm just gonna/going to take some Theraflu. — 我打算服用一些感冒藥水。
4. I have some Theraflu left in the kitchen cabinets. — 我廚房櫃子裡還有一些感冒藥水。
5. I went to the (running) track with Mia earlier. — 我剛剛跟Mia去操場跑步。
6. One second / One moment / Hold on / Hold up / Wait. — 等一下。
7. I met up with Charles at the lab. — 我在實驗室跟 Charles碰面。
8. We studied for the final (exam). — 我們有一起讀書準備期末考。
9. It's been a long day. — 今天真是漫長的一天（今天發生好多事，所以很疲憊）。
10. I'll help with the dirty dishes while you make dinner! — 我來幫忙洗碗，妳來做飯！
11. Who is on trash duty? — 今天是誰負責倒垃圾？
12. Let's just divide up the chores. — 但我們可以分配一下家務。
13. Take out the trash. — 倒垃圾。
14. I'll make us some spinach omelette. — 我來做菠菜歐姆蛋。
15. We'll have a pregame in the living room. — 我們星期五出去前可以在客廳來場小酌。
16. I was planning to switch up my room. — 我打算那時候重新佈置房間。
17. No biggies / No worries. — 沒什麼大不了的。
18. You do you. — 做自己。
19. The faucet's been acting up lately. — 那水龍頭最近又出狀況了。
20. Is it leaking again? — 又漏水了嗎？
21. Call the plumber. — 打電話叫水電工。
22. Adulting is hard. — 長大了真的不容易。
23. I feel you / I get you. — 我懂你。
24. On the bright side. — 往好的方面想。
25. Have a blast! — 玩得開心！

## Unit 3：餐廳點餐

1. What can I get started for you today? — 今天想要吃什麼？
2. Can I get a bowl? — 我要一份碗裝的餐點。
3. You got it. — 好的馬上做。
4. What kind of rice / beans / protein? — 你要什麼飯 / 豆子 / 蛋白質？
5. There's chicken, steak, carnitas, sofritas, and grilled veggies. — 我們有雞肉、牛排、燉豬肉、素肉和烤蔬菜。
6. Can you do chicken and veggies, half and half? — 可以雞肉和烤蔬菜各一半嗎？
7. Any salsa? Mild, medium, or hot? — 要哪種莎莎醬？溫和、中辣還是辣？
8. Guac is extra. — 酪梨醬需要額外收費。
9. Any chips and guac? — 要加玉米片和酪梨醬嗎？
10. Cash or card? — 付現金還是刷卡？
11. You're all set. — 完成囉！
12. Have a good one/day! — 祝你今天愉快！
13. You as well! — 你也是！
14. Which flavor is the most popular? — 哪一個口味最受歡迎？
15. Chocolate peanut butter cup and triple chocolate. — 巧克力花生醬杯和三重巧克力。
16. Can I get a box of six? — 我可以要六個裝一盒的嗎？
17. Let me get those packed for you. — 我幫你打包。
18. Alrighty / Alright. — 好的。
19. Did you need the receipt printed? — 需要列印收據嗎？
20. Be out in just a second. — 馬上為你送上！

## Unit 4：購物與結帳

1. Are you looking for anything specific/particular today? — 你今天有特別想找什麼嗎？
2. Just browsing/looking. — 只是隨便看看。
3. Let me know if you need help with anything. — 如果需要幫忙隨時告訴我。
4. Where is the fitting room? — 請問試衣間在哪裡？
5. Straight down the hallway to the right. — 沿著走廊直走，在右邊。
6. I'll go try it on. — 我去試穿看看。
7. I was hoping it comes in beige. — 我真希望有米色的款式。
8. Do you have this dress in a different color? OR Does this dress come in a different color? — 這件洋裝有其他顏色嗎？
9. I can help you with that. — 我可以幫你看看。
10. It is available online and in store. — 這款線上和門市都可以買到。
11. It comes in these colors. — 有這些顏色。
12. Are you looking for a going out dress or a (more) casual dress? — 妳是想找正式外出穿的洋裝還是比較休閒的洋裝？
13. Do you want a maxi dress like this one or midi or mini? — 你想要像這樣的長裙，還是中長裙或短裙？
14. Sleeveless, shortsleeves, longsleeves, strapless, or no preference? — （袖子要）無袖、短袖、長袖、平肩還是沒特別偏好？
15. One is a sleeveless denim maxi with the buttons, another one is a midi crew neck black and white striped knit dress. — 第一件是無袖的牛仔長裙，帶有釦子的設計；第二件是黑白條紋的圓領中長針織裙。
16. Where is the register? OR Where do I check out/pay? — 收銀台在哪裡？
17. Are you a member with us? — 您是我們的會員嗎？
18. Your total's gonna be $87.63 today. — 今天的總金額是87.63美金。
19. Cash or card? — 你要支付現金還是刷卡？
20. When you're ready, it'll ask you a question on the screen. — 等您準備好後，螢幕上會有問題讓您回答。
21. Did you need a bag? — 需要袋子嗎？
22. Do you want a copy of your receipt? / Would you like a receipt? — 需要收據嗎？

## Unit 5：住家維修

1. I'm calling because my AC is not working. — 我打電話來是因為我的冷氣壞了。
2. I was wondering when you guys can come out and check it. — 我想詢問你們什麼時候能來檢查一下？
3. It hasn't turned on since yesterday. — 它從昨天開始就沒有再啟動。
4. We'll send him in/out on Monday. — 我們會安排他週一過去。
5. The (AC) unit is out here in the backyard. — （冷氣）設備在後院這邊。
6. The compressor blew out and you are also low on refrigerant. — 壓縮機壞掉了，而且你的冷媒也快不足了。
7. The total before taxes is gonna be $5,325.25. — 稅前價格總共是5,325.25美金。
8. I listed all these problems out on an itemized estimate/statement. — 我把所有問題都列在一份有細項的估價單上了。
9. Holy moly! — 天啊！
10. It's gonna run into a lot of problems. — 未來可能會有很多問題。
11. I would recommend possibly looking at replacing your (AC) unit. — 我建議你考慮更換（冷氣）設備。
12. In the long run — 從長遠來看
13. I will send you this estimate for repairing your current (AC) unit. — 我會把修理目前（冷氣）設備的估價單傳給你。
14. I also noticed the thermostat seems off. — 我也發現恆溫器好像不正常。
15. It's not reading the temperature correctly. — 因為它沒有顯示正確的溫度。
16. Thanks for stopping by! — 謝謝你過來一趟！
17. Anytime / You're welcome! — 不客氣！

## Unit 6：旅行與住宿

1. What are you here for? — 你來這裡的目的是什麼？
2. I'm here for a vacation. — 我來這裡渡假。
3. Where are you staying? — 你要住在哪裡？
4. I'm staying at a hotel for two nights then staying with my old coworker. — 我會先住兩晚飯店，然後住在我的老同事家。
5. The address of the hotel is 216 Los Angeles Blvd. — 飯店的地址是洛杉磯大道216號。
6. How long do you plan to stay here? — 你預計在這裡待多久？
7. In total 32 days. The first 2 days at the hotel and the rest with my old coworker. — 總共32天，前兩天住飯店，之後都住在我老同事家。
8. How much money are you bringing in cash? — 你帶了多少現金？
9. A little under $2,000. — 不到 2,000 美金。
10. No need to declare it. — 不需要申報。
11. What is your profession? — 你的職業是什麼？
12. I am a marketing manager for a tech company. — 我是科技公司的行銷主管。
13. You're all set! — 都好了！
14. Can I see the credit card that you used for your reservation? — 我可以看一下您用來預訂的信用卡嗎？
15. Here you go. — 這邊給你。
16. Are we/you here to celebrate anything? — 我們來這是特別慶祝什麼嗎？
17. I was wondering if you have any suite upgrades available today? — 我想問一下今天有套房升級的選項嗎？
18. One moment, please. — 請稍等一下。
19. I upgraded you to an ocean view suite on the 33rd floor with a Jacuzzi. — 我已經幫您升級到33樓有按摩浴缸的海景套房。
20. You'll also get free high-speed WiFi. — 您還可以免費使用高速無線網絡。
21. Would you like a complimentary breakfast? — 您想要免費早餐嗎？
22. Or extra points for your stay? — 還是額外累計點數呢？
23. You got it! — 好的，沒問題。
24. The elevator is all the way across the lobby to the left. — 電梯在穿過大廳後的左邊。
25. Did you need one or two key cards? — 你需要一張還是兩張房卡？
26. Is there a gym here? — 這裡有健身房嗎？
27. What time does it open and close? (formal term: hours of operation) — 它幾點開放和關閉？（營業時間）
28. I'm gonna go check it out. — 我等會去看看。
29. There is a $20 per day resort fee. — 每天有20美金的度假村費用。
30. Anything else I can help you with? — 還有什麼我可以幫助您的嗎？

## Unit 7：健康與醫療

1. What brings you here today? — 今天哪裡不舒服？
2. I think I have a fever. — 我覺得我發燒了。
3. I will need your (picture) id. — 我需要您的身份證。
4. Date of birth? — 出生日期？
5. I also need the insurance card. — 我也需要你的醫療保險卡。
6. I have a picture of it. — 我有它的照片。
7. Fill out this top portion of the form, sign, and put down the date. — 請填寫表格的上半部分，簽名並填上日期。
8. Take a seat there and the doctor will call you. — 請在那邊坐一下，醫生會叫您。
9. I've been having a fever since yesterday, diarrhea too. — 我從昨天開始發燒，還有拉肚子。
10. At that point, I hadn't eaten anything for 30 hours. — 當時我已經大概30個小時沒吃東西了。
11. I started getting chills. — 我開始發冷。
12. Any exposure to someone with a flu? — 你是否有接觸有流感的人？
13. You're negative for covid, and also negative for the flu. — 你的 COVID 和流感檢測結果都是陰性的。
14. Is it associated with anything that you ate? — 會不會是跟你吃的東西有關？
15. You work from home, right? — 你是在家工作，對吧？
16. I just had some leftover pasta from a restaurant. — 我只是吃了餐廳的義大利麵剩菜。
17. You started feeling the symptoms right away? — 你馬上就開始感覺到症狀嗎？
18. Are you allergic to any medications? — 你有對任何藥物過敏嗎？
19. No, not that I know of. — 據我所知是沒有。
20. They took your blood pressure and temperature. — 他們有測了你的血壓和體溫。
21. Your heart rate is a bit elevated. — 你的心率有點偏高。
22. Not so much that we need to worry about. — 沒有什麼特別需要擔心的。
23. Give them a stool sample. — 給他們送糞便樣本。
24. We'll call you to let you know if you need any antibiotics. — 我們會打電話通知你是否需要抗生素。
25. Chills and headaches are primarily caused by dehydration. — 發冷和頭痛主要是因為脫水造成的。
26. In the meanwhile, try to stay away from spicy food, greasy food, and dairy. — 在此同時，盡量避免辛辣食物、油膩食物和乳製品。
27. If you want to get something over the counter, electrolyte drinks will help you a lot. — 如果你想要買藥房的非處方藥，電解質飲料對你會有很大的幫助。
28. You might need IV fluids. — 你可能會需要打點滴。
29. Feel better! — 希望你早日康復！

## Unit 8：客訴與抱怨

1. Hello. How can I help you? — 喂，請問有什麼可以協助您的？
2. I'm calling because the tire pressure light has been on. — 我打電話是因為輪胎氣壓燈亮了。
3. I did try to put air in the tires. — 我有嘗試幫輪胎打氣。
4. I didn't want to risk it. — 我不想冒險。
5. A 2-hour freeway drive — 兩小時的高速公路車程
6. Who am I speaking to? — 請問怎麼稱呼您？
7. I apologize for the inconvenience. — 很抱歉造成您的不便。
8. We will send someone to tow your car away. — 我們會派人去拖您的車。
9. Where are you located? — 您的位置在哪裡?
10. We are right down the ramp of a freeway. — 我們就在高速公路出口匝道的下面。
11. I see a Mcdonald's across the street. — 我看到對街有一家麥當勞。
12. What's the zip (code)? — 郵遞區號是什麼？
13. The tow truck is on the way. — 拖車已經派遣過去了。
14. But will the tow truck also drop off another car? — 但拖車會不會也送來另外一輛車？
15. They will only pick up the current car. — 他們只負責拖走您現有的車。
16. Our flight is taking off in 4 hours. — 我們的航班四個小時後就要起飛了。
17. We can send a driver to pick you up. — 我們可以派司機來接您。
18. That wouldn't work. — 那行不通。
19. We were cutting it close. — 我們時間已經很緊迫了。
20. I'm afraid not. — 沒辦法。
21. Will the cost be compensated? — 你們會補償費用嗎？
22. What would you suggest? — 你建議怎麼做？
23. We can get you a ride/taxi. — 我們可以為你安排計程車。
24. I will figure that part out. — 我會自己解決這部分。
25. I might have to miss my flight. — 我可能會錯過我的航班。
26. Do you compensate for hotel stays? — 你們會補償旅館住宿費用嗎？
27. You can talk to the branch manager. — 你可以和門市經理聯絡。

## Unit 9：交朋友閒聊

1. Do you know how to use this ticket machine? — 你知道怎麼用這個售票機嗎？
2. So it's your first time here in SF. / So is it your first time here in SF? — 所以這是你第一次來舊金山吧？
3. (I'm) just in town for a conference tomorrow. — 我來這裡參加一個明天的研討會。
4. What do you do? — 你是做什麼工作的？
5. What got you into that? — 是什麼讓你開始做這個的？
6. I am vegan so I enjoy cooking a lot. — 我吃素，所以我很喜歡煮飯。
7. I got so hooked on this tea. — 我迷上了這種茶。
8. It just went from there. — 事情就這樣發展起來了。
9. My stop is next. — 我下一站就到了。
10. What a story! — 真是個精彩的故事呀！
11. I'm gonna meet up with my friends to go rock climbing later. — 我待會要和朋友一起去攀岩。
12. Do you wanna join us? I'll text you (the time and location). — 你要不要加入我們？我可以等下訊息你（時間地點）。
13. You can take my instagram. — 你可以加我的 Instagram。
14. Cool cool / Cool beans. — 好喔好喔。

## Unit 12：生活分享、社交邀約與拒絕

### 生活分享 (Sharing about Life)

1. How's your day been? / How was work? / How was school? / How was the interview? — 今天過得怎麼樣？/ 工作如何？/ 學校怎麼樣？/ 面試怎麼樣？
2. It was great! / It's been alright. — 很好！/ 還行。
3. I had lunch late because I was in a meeting. — 我午餐吃得比較晚，因為在開會。
4. I didn't get to talk to Sally today because she was on vacation. — 我今天沒跟 Sally 聊到天，因為她去度假了。
5. I told my manager that I would want to switch to a different project. — 我告訴主管我想換到不同的工作項目上。
6. I got some groceries, put the gas, and dropped off products for return at UPS on the way back home. — 回家的路上我買了些超市的東西、加了油，把要退的貨送到了快遞公司 UPS。
7. I called my dad to let him know we're visiting him next month. — 我打了電話給爸爸告訴他我們下個月會去看他。
8. I took the leftovers to work today and got some Chinese takeout for dinner on the way home. — 我今天帶剩菜去公司吃，回家路上外帶了中式餐點當晚餐。
9. I parked my car far away because there was so much traffic coming back and all the parking spots (on our block) were taken. — 我把車停在很遠的地方，因為我回家的路上很塞車，（家裡附近街區的）停車位全滿了。
10. The train ran late so it took me extra long to get back. — 火車延誤了，所以我花了更長的時間才回來。
11. I finally handed in my essays and the professor said he'll meet with me next week to go over them. — 我終於交了論文報告，教授說下週會跟我約時間和我一起看過一遍。
12. I ran out of foundation and conditioner so I went to CVS (a pharmacy in the US) to grab some. — 我的粉底和護髮乳用完了，所以去美國的藥妝店CVS買了一些。
13. What is your plan for this weekend? — 你週末有什麼計劃嗎？

### 邀約他人 (Inviting Others)

14. Do you want to join us this Friday to the party? — 你想這週五跟我們一起參加派對嗎？
15. We're going on a hike tomorrow. Would you like to come? — 我們明天要去爬山。你想一起來嗎？

17. Are you available Friday afternoon? We're going to check out this new cheesesteak place down the street! — 你這週五下午有空嗎？我們打算去這條街上的新起司牛排店吃看看！
18. My sister is visiting next weekend. Would you want to join us to a play next Sunday? — 我姐姐下週末來訪。你想週日跟我們一起去看戲嗎？
19. We're going to the beach tomorrow. If you're free, you should come hang with us! — 我們明天要去海灘。如果你有空，你應該一起加入我們！
20. My friend is playing at this jazz bar Saturday night. You should come with me for some music and great food! — 我朋友週六晚上會在爵士酒吧演奏音樂。你應該跟我一起去享受音樂和美食！

### 拒絕邀約 (Turning Down Invitations)

21. Thanks for inviting me but I'm going to have to take a rain check this time. — 感謝你的邀請，但這次我得改天再跟你約。
22. I would love to but I have to get this essay turned in tomorrow. — 我很想去，但我明天得交這篇論文。
23. I appreciate the invitation but I have a family gathering this weekend. — 感謝你的邀請，但這週末我有個家庭聚會。
24. I'm so sorry but I won't be able to make it this time. — 非常抱歉，但這次我沒辦法參加。
25. This sounds wonderful. But I'll have to pass this time. — 這聽起來很棒，但這次我得放棄了。
26. I wish I could join but I have to take care of a few things that day. — 我真希望能參加，但那天我有些事情需要處理。
27. Thanks for the invite, but I'll have to sit this one out. — 感謝你的邀請，但這次我得缺席了。
28. I'm afraid I have to stay in that evening to catch up with some work. Maybe next time! — 那天晚上我恐怕得待在家趕些工作，下次再約吧！
29. I'd love to go, but my schedule won't allow it this time. — 我很想去，但這次我的行程不允許。
30. Ah, I'll have to miss out this time. But have fun! — 啊，我這次得錯過了。但祝你玩得開心！
