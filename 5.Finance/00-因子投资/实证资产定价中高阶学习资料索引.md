## 一、Koijen & Yogo — 需求系统资产定价（DSAP）

### 核心论文
- **A Demand System Approach to Asset Pricing** (JPE, 2019)
  - https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/full/10.1086/701683
  - PDF: `Resources/papers/Koijen_Yogo_Demand_System_JPE.pdf`

- Asset Embeddings (Gabaix, Koijen, Richmond, Yogo, 2023)
  - https://dx.doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.4507511
- Exchange Rates and Asset Prices in a Global Demand System (2020)
  - https://www.nber.org/papers/w27342
- Limited Risk Transfer Between Investors (Gabaix, Koijen, Mainardi, Oh, Yogo, 2025)
  - https://dx.doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.5074997
- On the Theory and Econometrics of DSAP (Koijen & Yogo, June 2025)
  - https://dx.doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.5274709

## 二、Stefan Nagel — 机器学习与资产定价

### 核心书籍
- **Machine Learning in Asset Pricing** (Princeton University Press, 2021)
  - https://press.princeton.edu/books/hardcover/9780691218700
  - IEEE Xplore: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/book/9519805
  - ⚠️ 商业出版，需购买（约 $45），暂只有预览版

### 讲座幻灯片
- **Princeton Lectures in Finance 2019** — 后来扩展成书的基础
  - Day 1: `Resources/papers/Nagel_PrincetonLectures_Day1.pdf`
  - Day 2: `Resources/papers/Nagel_PrincetonLectures_Day2.pdf`
  - 下载页: https://voices.uchicago.edu/stefannagel/discussions/

- **Seemingly Virtuous Complexity in Return Prediction** (2025年7月修订)

- 主观信念与资产定价系列演讲:

## 三、Tobias Moskowitz — 量化投资课程

### Yale SOM 课程
- **MGT 595: Quantitative Investing** (Yale School of Management)
  - https://faculty.som.yale.edu/tobymoskowitz/research/teaching/
