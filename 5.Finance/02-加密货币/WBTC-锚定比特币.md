## 概念

WBTC 是 **Wrapped Bitcoin** 的缩写，意为“锚定比特币”。它是一种 ERC-20 代币，与比特币（BTC）按 1:1 的比例锚定，运行在以太坊区块链上[[1](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQHIkKzbCiW2A0zNbJClyJxJvADY_QiRntUfVDdJA5oCtcK2PTt--ig0m1ERIZ2olgNzhhudnULuBiZsx7zHecOeC3Eq8dEnFimjJ_CPru0CH74KMPsJvdObS-g9_WD9EeG2Kf98tZ9H4Q17w2rQZR-SeHBOqWcbNO5u1-jK)][[2](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQEFij7urL4sE8MND0KKJETzbSMjuO19S_JwORtZNXIdcTTx_tkoHZBzB8C97vJWxCfQRX9Sye8aS7fliKg_ktYcWve0jH6SPBAHc6rRouDkxsEFrVo9MqwguZQTdq7tgrablg35Np9YGi-B3-9Vau_zArwPPurK5e4InQ%3D%3D)][[3](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQH718dcvqwIrPXqX03jKV9HsI6Bn3hg1vxvxL5Fj5E3hGU7w8Sr45YXhtD1KsoyohQ4MEjW6sqRZv9LAgstYrYjAIk7Wi0PTJ5wTOQCSTAEuSPo5UK_TJpQ4Sq6PUz8VQKL5A_uYa14pIahqkSkVXm7NlIONrxfNemJR_fuhjv8Fpvze5dBQd7UavW1zg%3D%3D)][[4](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQGvDJwyvYP4Kc6oct9hpl8ksOPRmwLUlmOXEVj5wkIjuDiUtvskwr2pgUcUWKIqnieSE4Qb-hAdmEwaimIEpLy3VmIEq6HqVmsC4U3msXw545Undg5CyDenMWuKq8m-uvRnW-Z5xYbQinrE41TVPF_gDd3uXTxlkIM%3D)]。

## **WBTC 的作用和目的**

比特币和以太坊是两个独立的区块链，它们之间通常无法直接进行交互。比特币本身不能在以太坊网络上的去中心化应用（dApps）中使用，因为它不符合以太坊的 ERC-20 代币标准[[1](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQHIkKzbCiW2A0zNbJClyJxJvADY_QiRntUfVDdJA5oCtcK2PTt--ig0m1ERIZ2olgNzhhudnULuBiZsx7zHecOeC3Eq8dEnFimjJ_CPru0CH74KMPsJvdObS-g9_WD9EeG2Kf98tZ9H4Q17w2rQZR-SeHBOqWcbNO5u1-jK)][[4](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQGvDJwyvYP4Kc6oct9hpl8ksOPRmwLUlmOXEVj5wkIjuDiUtvskwr2pgUcUWKIqnieSE4Qb-hAdmEwaimIEpLy3VmIEq6HqVmsC4U3msXw545Undg5CyDenMWuKq8m-uvRnW-Z5xYbQinrE41TVPF_gDd3uXTxlkIM%3D)][[5](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQHFGezK5yeDtM5CIpulF4HxJHmrkJ60bZjF7INWkoE7UX3DkMIqOzH80xmJN2fdIllqyO4-qbAvJjvqO7OIWupMW5skQZnXII68vG9Nhxo3_iFutthfwZTFHp8V2I3w9Cf3ESHKFxGMmtG2sHVXWBt7XhG_isS-xXyLdUls14q5Mv3cYKQbRgUEAiO8EvxNhkD1MfgWJQEDB_zUMR6oSVaM)]。WBTC 的出现解决了这个问题，它允许比特币持有者在不放弃比特币价值的情况下，参与到以太坊日益增长的去中心化金融（DeFi）生态系统中[[1](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQHIkKzbCiW2A0zNbJClyJxJvADY_QiRntUfVDdJA5oCtcK2PTt--ig0m1ERIZ2olgNzhhudnULuBiZsx7zHecOeC3Eq8dEnFimjJ_CPru0CH74KMPsJvdObS-g9_WD9EeG2Kf98tZ9H4Q17w2rQZR-SeHBOqWcbNO5u1-jK)][[2](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQEFij7urL4sE8MND0KKJETzbSMjuO19S_JwORtZNXIdcTTx_tkoHZBzB8C97vJWxCfQRX9Sye8aS7fliKg_ktYcWve0jH6SPBAHc6rRouDkxsEFrVo9MqwguZQTdq7tgrablg35Np9YGi-B3-9Vau_zArwPPurK5e4InQ%3D%3D)][[4](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQGvDJwyvYP4Kc6oct9hpl8ksOPRmwLUlmOXEVj5wkIjuDiUtvskwr2pgUcUWKIqnieSE4Qb-hAdmEwaimIEpLy3VmIEq6HqVmsC4U3msXw545Undg5CyDenMWuKq8m-uvRnW-Z5xYbQinrE41TVPF_gDd3uXTxlkIM%3D)][[6](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQHdU65KEMAh4gZsElR7jFPr61CDYceQAuK0s6otqum8kyo1iBIOngZ8piyKYuNWcLm7luO9eK0SeEvgT11oBB4pLC0Y98dK4nzQ_7yNdTNRY2BBEnUgzpX-_OK4PEvoa4s6Soc8E24UOFFd_GTVlb0kTCPMEw%3D%3D)]。

## **WBTC 的工作原理**

1. **抵押 (Wrapping)：** 当用户想要获得 WBTC 时，他们会将等量的比特币存入一个由托管方（例如 BitGo Trust）管理的储备金中[[1](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQHIkKzbCiW2A0zNbJClyJxJvADY_QiRntUfVDdJA5oCtcK2PTt--ig0m1ERIZ2olgNzhhudnULuBiZsx7zHecOeC3Eq8dEnFimjJ_CPru0CH74KMPsJvdObS-g9_WD9EeG2Kf98tZ9H4Q17w2rQZR-SeHBOqWcbNO5u1-jK)][[2](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQEFij7urL4sE8MND0KKJETzbSMjuO19S_JwORtZNXIdcTTx_tkoHZBzB8C97vJWxCfQRX9Sye8aS7fliKg_ktYcWve0jH6SPBAHc6rRouDkxsEFrVo9MqwguZQTdq7tgrablg35Np9YGi-B3-9Vau_zArwPPurK5e4InQ%3D%3D)][[6](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQHdU65KEMAh4gZsElR7jFPr61CDYceQAuK0s6otqum8kyo1iBIOngZ8piyKYuNWcLm7luO9eK0SeEvgT11oBB4pLC0Y98dK4nzQ_7yNdTNRY2BBEnUgzpX-_OK4PEvoa4s6Soc8E24UOFFd_GTVlb0kTCPMEw%3D%3D)]。
2. **铸造 (Minting)：** 托管方收到比特币后，会在以太坊区块链上铸造等量的 WBTC 代币，并将其发送给用户或商家[[2](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQEFij7urL4sE8MND0KKJETzbSMjuO19S_JwORtZNXIdcTTx_tkoHZBzB8C97vJWxCfQRX9Sye8aS7fliKg_ktYcWve0jH6SPBAHc6rRouDkxsEFrVo9MqwguZQTdq7tgrablg35Np9YGi-B3-9Vau_zArwPPurK5e4InQ%3D%3D)][[3](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQH718dcvqwIrPXqX03jKV9HsI6Bn3hg1vxvxL5Fj5E3hGU7w8Sr45YXhtD1KsoyohQ4MEjW6sqRZv9LAgstYrYjAIk7Wi0PTJ5wTOQCSTAEuSPo5UK_TJpQ4Sq6PUz8VQKL5A_uYa14pIahqkSkVXm7NlIONrxfNemJR_fuhjv8Fpvze5dBQd7UavW1zg%3D%3D)]。
3. **1:1 锚定：** 每铸造一个 WBTC，都会有等量的比特币被锁定在储备金中，从而确保 WBTC 的价值始终与比特币保持 1:1 的锚定关系[[1](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQHIkKzbCiW2A0zNbJClyJxJvADY_QiRntUfVDdJA5oCtcK2PTt--ig0m1ERIZ2olgNzhhudnULuBiZsx7zHecOeC3Eq8dEnFimjJ_CPru0CH74KMPsJvdObS-g9_WD9EeG2Kf98tZ9H4Q17w2rQZR-SeHBOqWcbNO5u1-jK)][[2](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQEFij7urL4sE8MND0KKJETzbSMjuO19S_JwORtZNXIdcTTx_tkoHZBzB8C97vJWxCfQRX9Sye8aS7fliKg_ktYcWve0jH6SPBAHc6rRouDkxsEFrVo9MqwguZQTdq7tgrablg35Np9YGi-B3-9Vau_zArwPPurK5e4InQ%3D%3D)][[4](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQGvDJwyvYP4Kc6oct9hpl8ksOPRmwLUlmOXEVj5wkIjuDiUtvskwr2pgUcUWKIqnieSE4Qb-hAdmEwaimIEpLy3VmIEq6HqVmsC4U3msXw545Undg5CyDenMWuKq8m-uvRnW-Z5xYbQinrE41TVPF_gDd3uXTxlkIM%3D)][[6](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQHdU65KEMAh4gZsElR7jFPr61CDYceQAuK0s6otqum8kyo1iBIOngZ8piyKYuNWcLm7luO9eK0SeEvgT11oBB4pLC0Y98dK4nzQ_7yNdTNRY2BBEnUgzpX-_OK4PEvoa4s6Soc8E24UOFFd_GTVlb0kTCPMEw%3D%3D)]。
4. **赎回 (Unwrapping)：** 当用户想要将 WBTC 换回比特币时，他们会将 WBTC 发送给托管方，托管方会销毁这些 WBTC，并从储备金中释放等量的比特币给用户[[1](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQHIkKzbCiW2A0zNbJClyJxJvADY_QiRntUfVDdJA5oCtcK2PTt--ig0m1ERIZ2olgNzhhudnULuBiZsx7zHecOeC3Eq8dEnFimjJ_CPru0CH74KMPsJvdObS-g9_WD9EeG2Kf98tZ9H4Q17w2rQZR-SeHBOqWcbNO5u1-jK)]。

## **WBTC 的主要优势**

- **DeFi 互操作性：** 允许比特币持有者参与以太坊上的各种 DeFi 应用，例如借贷、抵押、流动性挖矿和在去中心化交易所（DEX）上交易等[[2](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQEFij7urL4sE8MND0KKJETzbSMjuO19S_JwORtZNXIdcTTx_tkoHZBzB8C97vJWxCfQRX9Sye8aS7fliKg_ktYcWve0jH6SPBAHc6rRouDkxsEFrVo9MqwguZQTdq7tgrablg35Np9YGi-B3-9Vau_zArwPPurK5e4InQ%3D%3D)][[4](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQGvDJwyvYP4Kc6oct9hpl8ksOPRmwLUlmOXEVj5wkIjuDiUtvskwr2pgUcUWKIqnieSE4Qb-hAdmEwaimIEpLy3VmIEq6HqVmsC4U3msXw545Undg5CyDenMWuKq8m-uvRnW-Z5xYbQinrE41TVPF_gDd3uXTxlkIM%3D)][[6](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQHdU65KEMAh4gZsElR7jFPr61CDYceQAuK0s6otqum8kyo1iBIOngZ8piyKYuNWcLm7luO9eK0SeEvgT11oBB4pLC0Y98dK4nzQ_7yNdTNRY2BBEnUgzpX-_OK4PEvoa4s6Soc8E24UOFFd_GTVlb0kTCPMEw%3D%3D)]。
- **增加流动性：** 将比特币的巨大市值和流动性引入到以太坊的 DeFi 生态系统，为 DeFi 协议提供更多资金[[1](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQHIkKzbCiW2A0zNbJClyJxJvADY_QiRntUfVDdJA5oCtcK2PTt--ig0m1ERIZ2olgNzhhudnULuBiZsx7zHecOeC3Eq8dEnFimjJ_CPru0CH74KMPsJvdObS-g9_WD9EeG2Kf98tZ9H4Q17w2rQZR-SeHBOqWcbNO5u1-jK)][[2](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQEFij7urL4sE8MND0KKJETzbSMjuO19S_JwORtZNXIdcTTx_tkoHZBzB8C97vJWxCfQRX9Sye8aS7fliKg_ktYcWve0jH6SPBAHc6rRouDkxsEFrVo9MqwguZQTdq7tgrablg35Np9YGi-B3-9Vau_zArwPPurK5e4InQ%3D%3D)][[4](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQGvDJwyvYP4Kc6oct9hpl8ksOPRmwLUlmOXEVj5wkIjuDiUtvskwr2pgUcUWKIqnieSE4Qb-hAdmEwaimIEpLy3VmIEq6HqVmsC4U3msXw545Undg5CyDenMWuKq8m-uvRnW-Z5xYbQinrE41TVPF_gDd3uXTxlkIM%3D)][[6](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQHdU65KEMAh4gZsElR7jFPr61CDYceQAuK0s6otqum8kyo1iBIOngZ8piyKYuNWcLm7luO9eK0SeEvgT11oBB4pLC0Y98dK4nzQ_7yNdTNRY2BBEnUgzpX-_OK4PEvoa4s6Soc8E24UOFFd_GTVlb0kTCPMEw%3D%3D)]。
- **交易速度：** WBTC 交易在以太坊区块链上结算，比比特币网络上的交易速度更快，因为以太坊出块时间更短[[2](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQEFij7urL4sE8MND0KKJETzbSMjuO19S_JwORtZNXIdcTTx_tkoHZBzB8C97vJWxCfQRX9Sye8aS7fliKg_ktYcWve0jH6SPBAHc6rRouDkxsEFrVo9MqwguZQTdq7tgrablg35Np9YGi-B3-9Vau_zArwPPurK5e4InQ%3D%3D)]。
- **透明度：** WBTC 的发行量是公开的，并且可以验证底层的比特币储备金，尽管其本质上仍是一种中心化的解决方案，依赖于托管方的信任[[1](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQHIkKzbCiW2A0zNbJClyJxJvADY_QiRntUfVDdJA5oCtcK2PTt--ig0m1ERIZ2olgNzhhudnULuBiZsx7zHecOeC3Eq8dEnFimjJ_CPru0CH74KMPsJvdObS-g9_WD9EeG2Kf98tZ9H4Q17w2rQZR-SeHBOqWcbNO5u1-jK)][[5](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQHFGezK5yeDtM5CIpulF4HxJHmrkJ60bZjF7INWkoE7UX3DkMIqOzH80xmJN2fdIllqyO4-qbAvJjvqO7OIWupMW5skQZnXII68vG9Nhxo3_iFutthfwZTFHp8V2I3w9Cf3ESHKFxGMmtG2sHVXWBt7XhG_isS-xXyLdUls14q5Mv3cYKQbRgUEAiO8EvxNhkD1MfgWJQEDB_zUMR6oSVaM)][[6](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&q=https%3A%2F%2Fvertexaisearch.cloud.google.com%2Fgrounding-api-redirect%2FAUZIYQHdU65KEMAh4gZsElR7jFPr61CDYceQAuK0s6otqum8kyo1iBIOngZ8piyKYuNWcLm7luO9eK0SeEvgT11oBB4pLC0Y98dK4nzQ_7yNdTNRY2BBEnUgzpX-_OK4PEvoa4s6Soc8E24UOFFd_GTVlb0kTCPMEw%3D%3D)]。

总而言之，WBTC 就像是比特币在以太坊上的“替身”，它让比特币能够在以太坊的智能合约环境中发挥作用，为比特币持有者解锁了更多金融服务的可能性。
