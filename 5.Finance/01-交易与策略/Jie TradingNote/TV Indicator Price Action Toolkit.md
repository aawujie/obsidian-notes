## 概念

TradingView 指标名称：Price Action Toolkit (PAT) 

> [PAT Screener | Flux Charts — 指标由fluxchart提供](https://cn.tradingview.com/script/w0dsFfR5-PAT-Screener-Flux-Charts/)

![[imgs/image 202.png]]

价格行为学主要特征：

1. Finds Latest Across 8 Tickers
2. Order Blocks
3. Breaker Blocks
4. Fair Value Gaps (FVG)
5. Inversion FVGs
6. Market Structures (BOS, CHoCH, CHoCH+)
7. Liquidity Zones
8. Liquidity Grabs
9. Premium / Discount Zones

显示额外附加信息：

10. Strength
11. Retests
12. (Bullish & Bearish) Volume
13. Consumption
